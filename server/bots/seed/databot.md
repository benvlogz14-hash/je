# Stringer Media x Euclidean Media: Shorts Proposal

## The offer: $1,200/mo
- **At least 6 Shorts a day,** each cross-posted to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels, with its own caption for each platform.
- Built from your footage and your best long-form moments, plus recuts of your old hits.
- **First week free,** no card. **You approve everything** before it goes up.

## What we found in your last 120 Shorts
- Label-style titles ("Abandoned X in Y") get about **half the views** of story titles (3.9K vs 7.35K median).
- Full-video links are buried in descriptions, and many Shorts have none.
- Runtimes run **60 to 103 seconds.**
- Only **1 in 4 Shorts breaks 10K** views.

## Goals
- **Target:** lift your average YouTube Shorts views above **10K**, up from today's 5.9K median. This is a goal, not a guarantee.
- **What's holding the Shorts back:** almost all run about a minute, there's no visual hook, and the titles are generic. Fixing those three things could potentially double your Shorts views.
- **The missing link:** your Shorts don't use YouTube's Related video link back to the long-form, and a link in the description is easy to miss. Linking every new Short to its long-form could potentially double the long-form views that come from Shorts.
- **Reviving old videos:** new Shorts cut from older long-form videos and linked back to them can bring videos that have gone quiet in the algorithm back to life.

## The plan
- **Shock-first hooks:** the craziest object on screen in second 1, with under 8 words of on-screen text.
- **Motion and real sound on frame 1.**
- **Lowercase story titles under 70 characters:** one specific detail plus a mystery.
- **Tighter 30 to 50 second cuts** built from your best long-form moments.
- **Related video link on 100% of Shorts,** pointing to the matching full video.
- **Captions per platform:** TikTok gets search keywords; Instagram gets a hook first line and 5 hashtags max.
- **Judged by Stayed to watch,** the share of viewers who don't swipe away, not just views.

## Hook tactics
**Use:** door push reveals, flashlight snaps, POV entries, a hand on the object, push-ins, sound hits, speed ramps (speed up the walk, slow the reveal), 4 to 6 word text hooks in the upper third, and about 20% punch-ins to cover cuts.
**Avoid:** still openers, big centered text, fancy transitions, dead air on the walk-up, and reposting duplicates.
**5-minute hook pack at every location:** 3 door pushes, 1 flashlight snap, 1 POV entry, 2 hand-on-object shots, and 1 slow push-in on the best detail.

## The money reality
Shorts pay almost nothing directly: an estimated $30 to $100 a month for you. They are a growth play. Their job is to bring in subscribers and send viewers to your long-form, which is where your revenue is.

## Optional next step, after the Shorts push: long-form
Not the main ask. Once the Shorts are working, we can talk about a **$2,000/mo full package:** long-form editing, titles, scripting, thumbnails with A/B tests, and recuts of the first 30 seconds of each video.
- **Where you are now:** 688K subscribers, about 4.9M views a month, a long-form median of about 84K views.
- **Estimate, not a promise:** one extra median long-form a week is about 4 more a month, an estimated $1.7K to $4.4K a month in revenue. One hit could cover months of fees.
- **What we would improve:** title and thumbnail testing, stronger first 30 seconds, chapter markers, and more editing capacity so you can post more often.

**Track record:** For a former creator we worked with, we rebuilt how their Shorts were hooked, titled and cut, and their Shorts performance improved.
