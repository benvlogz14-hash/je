# Euclidean

Desktop app for Euclidean Media (founders Ryan Rosario and Ben Gingold), a short-form video agency for creators and political campaigns. It runs clients, footage, clip production, approvals, messaging, publishing, analytics and AI agents in one place.

## How it's built
- **Electron shell:** `app/main.js` and `app/preload.js`. The engine runs inside it; `ctx` hooks include `pickFolder`, `pickFiles`, `relaunch` and `revealFile`.
- **Node engine:** `server/index.js` and `server/api.js` (a custom Router: `r.get/post/patch/del(path, (req, body, params) => ..., { roles, stream, raw })`).
  - JSON store: `server/core/store.js` (`ctx.store.all/get/find/insert/update/remove`).
  - Audit log: `server/core/audit.js`.
  - Server-sent events: `server/core/events.js`.
- **Jobs:** `server/jobs/queue.js` and `server/jobs/handlers.js`. Every automated step is a `queue.register(name, { title, run(job, h) })` handler; `h.progress(frac, text)` reports progress.
- **AI cost order (default, set once on existing installs via `_costModeV1`):** `ai.local = 'all'` and `ai.bridge.use = 'first'`: the founder's own Grok/Claude/ChatGPT login (rate-limited, only once logged in on the AI apps page), then free Llama, then paid API keys.
- **AI:** `server/integrations/ai.js`. Use `ai.ask({ role, system, prompt, json, search, clientId, jobId })`; it routes by role to Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI with fallback, budgets and usage tracking. `search: true` gives live web search on xAI only.
- **Messaging:** `server/automation/messaging.js`.
  - `createOutbound(...)` puts AI drafts and first contact with prospects into Approvals.
  - `deliver` re-checks opt-outs, quiet hours, the hourly cap and Test mode before sending.
  - In Test mode, mail goes to the sandbox address.
  - Email is SMTP/IMAP (`server/integrations/email.js`); texts and calls use Twilio (`server/integrations/twilio.js`).
- **Video:**
  - FFmpeg helpers: `server/video/ffmpeg.js`.
  - Studio exporter with 47 xfade transitions, keyframes and ASS titles: `server/video/studio.js`.
  - **Text animations** (title designer → Entrance/Exit, or Studio → Effects → Text animations for every selected title/caption at once): in = Fade, Pop, Slide up, Drop in, Zoom in, Blur in, Typewriter; out = Fade out, Pop out, Slide down, Zoom out, Blur out, Type away (`text.animIn` / `text.animOut`; `textAnim` in both studio.js files, same numbers; export: `animTags` on the first/last subtitle piece, an exit gets its own last 0.3 s piece, keyframed titles frame by frame). **Typing sound** (`text.typeSound`, default on, `text.typeVol` 0-2; on/off and a Typing volume slider 0-200% in Effect controls for any typewriter title, and in the title designer; each change plays a click at that volume): a key click per letter (softer typing away), synthesized in code: preview with Web Audio as letters appear during playback (`typeClick`, `typedCount`), export as one WAV per title (`typingTrack` + `typeHits`) mixed in at the title's start. Tested: export types the letters, pops out at the end, clicks in the sound. A new title is selected right after it's added. **Motion** presets (Effects → Motion: Slow zoom in / out = scale keys 100→115% over the clip, Punch-in = 118% for 1.5 s at the playhead). **Markers**: M adds/removes one at the playhead (`seq.markers`), ; and ' jump to the previous/next, click one on the ruler to jump, right-click removes; the playhead and clips snap to them.
  - **Effect layers** (like Premiere's adjustment layers; clip `type: 5`, `FX_LAYER` in studio.js): Studio → Effects → New effect layer, or pick any effect with nothing selected; it's placed over the whole edit on the top video track (a new "Effects" track if that's busy) and selected. Its effects apply to everything on the tracks below it while it's on the timeline (trim it to cover part of the edit); titles and captions are never affected. Opacity sets the layer's strength. Preview: `drawFxLayer` copies the canvas drawn so far, runs it through the layer's filters and retro looks, and lays it back at that opacity. Export (`fxLayer` in `server/video/studio.js`): the composite so far is split, the layer's span trimmed, filtered (effect time from the layer's start, like the preview), given alpha and overlaid back. Tested: Noir + VHS layer at 80% over clips on two tracks, untouched before and after.
  - **Preview playback** (`Monitor`, shared state `PB` in studio.js; Nov 7 rewrite after Ryan: "always repeats audio, plays a few seconds after pause, often cuts to black"): **the clock follows the sound**: the master player (first audible audio clip, else the topmost video; `pickMaster`) is never pulled back, so sound never repeats; the clock waits for it to start or finish buffering (0.4 s at a start, held on the clip's own start so a cut's next clip actually starts; 1.5 s mid-play, then it carries on without it), jumps forward when it runs ahead, and the picture and other players follow (videos nudged ±8%, seek past 0.5 s; other audio ±5%, seek past 0.3 s). Before, the clock was the wall clock, held still whenever a video buffered while the sound played on, and the sound was then dragged back (the repeats). While playing a frame up to 1 s off is drawn (it was 0.35 s, and videos 0.35-0.6 s behind were skipped: the black flashes) and the last picture holds up to 1.2 s while a video seeks. **No re-draw while playing**: the loop writes the time to `PB` and the timeline playhead, its timecode and the monitor's timecode move straight on the page (`onPlayhead`; the timeline scrolls a page when the playhead reaches its edge, sizes cached so there's no forced layout); the editor gets the time once, on pause. Its `t` is kept current during playback (`PB.editorT`, rounded to a frame) and the keyboard handler re-binds every draw, so Ctrl+B, paste, titles and markers during playback land on the live playhead; `setT` while playing seeks (`PB.seek`). **Pause is instant**: `setPlaying(false)` calls `PB.stop()` first, which pauses every player before anything re-draws, and hands back the master's own time. Clip lanes are their own memoized component (`TimelineLanes`, handlers through a ref), so scrubbing and frame steps don't re-draw hundreds of clips; ruler scrubbing updates at most once per screen frame. Tested (60 s video with a click every second, 15 jump cuts, 300 captions, CPU slowed 6x): black frames 29% → 0, sound jumping back (0.62 s) → never, clock 4.4 s behind after 20 s → on time, picture-to-sound gap median 0.125 s / max 0.5 s → 0.011 / 0.048 s, scrub work 5.2 → 2.9 s; Ctrl+B mid-play cut exactly on the playhead's frame.
  - **Export errors say why** (Nov 13): the job's message is FFmpeg's first real error (`queue.ffmpegReason`; it used to show only the last line, "Conversion failed!"), and a Studio export whose graphics-card encoder (NVENC/QSV/AMF) fails is made again once with libx264 (a warning says so; working exports unchanged). Clip-picking replies that copy the format's hints ("<=60 chars, curiosity-driven" as a title) are dropped by `intelligence.unecho`, so the clip keeps its normal title/hook/caption.
  - **Retro looks** (Studio → Effects → Retro, like CapCut's filters): VHS, 90s Camcorder, Y2K Digicam, Super 8, Old Film, Glitch, CRT TV, Noir, Night Vision, 8-bit, each with Intensity % (`mix`, 1-100: the look laid over the untouched picture; export blends a copy with `split` + `blend`, the preview draws the plain picture then the look at that alpha), Strength (`amount`) (VHS also has **Rolling bar %** (`band`, 0-100, 0 = off; missing = 100 so older projects look the same, Nov 13, Ryan: "a slider for that line that goes across the screen") for the grey tracking bar), (and a Date stamp switch on VHS/Camcorder/Digicam: PLAY + date, REC + tape counter + date, orange digicam date, in VT323). Moving boxes (VHS rolling bar, Old Film scratch, Glitch bars) are drawn in exports with an empty `drawtext` box (`movingBox`: `boxw`/`boxh`, FFmpeg 6.1+, checked once with `-h filter=drawtext`; left out on older FFmpeg), because drawbox's `t` is its line thickness, not the time, so before Nov 13 those boxes only showed in the preview and never in exports (tested: the bar at the expected row, 40% fainter, 0 gone; the scratch at the expected column; the glitch bar only in its window). Export: plain FFmpeg filter chains in `server/video/retro.js` (rgbashift, noise, drawgrid scanlines, drawbox tracking band/scratch/glitch blocks, curves, colorbalance, drawtext); preview: `renderer/js/lib/retro.js` (CSS filters, an SVG RGB-split filter, grain tile, scanlines, the same bands/stamps) with the same numbers. Stamps and flashes go in the part of the clip that's on screen (`onScreen` in studio.js; a wide video filling a tall frame has its sides off screen). Written from scratch, no third-party code.
  - AI edit operations: `server/video/edit-ops.js`.
  - Preview proxies: `server/video/proxy.js`.
  - Looking through video: `server/video/vision.js` (`look`, `cached`, `summarize`, `shotAt`). FFmpeg finds the shots, grabs a still per shot, measures brightness, sharpness and motion; faces are counted; an AI that can see (`ai.ask({ images })`, Claude/GPT/Grok, or the free `llama3.2-vision` via Ollama) describes each shot (kind, what, on-screen text, quality, usable). Runs in the `analyze` job (setting `video.lookThrough`) and on demand (`video.look`, Production → Footage → Look through / Shots); feeds clip discovery and the speech ads' B-roll and line picking.
- **Copy a video's style** (a small option in Growth → Politics → Package settings; `server/video/reverse.js`, job `video.reverse`, `/api/reverse`, `/api/reverse/upload`): any video link or uploaded file. Measures cuts, pacing, colours and loudness, looks through it shot by shot, and an AI that can see writes a recipe. "Use for the political ads" sets `political.styleNotes` (fed to the ad writer) and the pacing (`political.reference`).

Personal-account terms limit automated use, so it's off by default.

## Crash safety
- Each page is wrapped in `PageGuard` (`renderer/js/main.js`, a Preact error boundary): an error while drawing one page shows "This page hit a problem" on that page (Try again / Go to Messages, which also forgets the remembered Production client) instead of taking down the app. `boot` only shows "Could not reach the Euclidean engine" when `/api/bootstrap` actually fails.
- Production ignores a remembered client (`eu.prodClient` in localStorage) that no longer exists; the Video style switch read `client.name` of a deleted/archived client and crashed the whole app at start.
