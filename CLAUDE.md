# Euclidean

Desktop app for Euclidean Media (founders Ryan Rosario and Ben Gingold), a short-form video agency for creators and political campaigns. It runs clients, footage, clip production, approvals, messaging, publishing, analytics and AI agents in one place.

## How it's built
- **Electron shell:** `app/main.js` and `app/preload.js`. The engine runs inside it; `ctx` hooks include `pickFolder`, `pickFiles`, `relaunch` and `revealFile`.
- **Node engine:** `server/index.js` and `server/api.js` (a custom Router: `r.get/post/patch/del(path, (req, body, params) => ..., { roles, stream, raw })`).
  - JSON store: `server/core/store.js` (`ctx.store.all/get/find/insert/update/remove`).
  - Audit log: `server/core/audit.js`.
  - Server-sent events: `server/core/events.js`.
- **Jobs:** `server/jobs/queue.js` and `server/jobs/handlers.js`. Every automated step is a `queue.register(name, { title, run(job, h) })` handler; `h.progress(frac, text)` reports progress.
- **AI cost order (default, set once on existing installs via `_costModeV1`):** `ai.local = 'all'` and `ai.bridge.use = 'first'`: the founder's own Grok/Claude/ChatGPT login (rate-limited, only once logged in on the AI apps page), then free Llama, then paid API keys.
- **AI:** `server/integrations/ai.js`. Use `ai.ask({ role, system, prompt, json, search, clientId, jobId })`; it routes by role to Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI with fallback, budgets and usage tracking. `search: true` gives live web search on xAI only.
- **Messaging:** `server/automation/messaging.js`.
  - `createOutbound(...)` puts AI drafts and first contact with prospects into Approvals.
  - `deliver` re-checks opt-outs, quiet hours, the hourly cap and Test mode before sending.
  - In Test mode, mail goes to the sandbox address.
  - Email is SMTP/IMAP (`server/integrations/email.js`); texts and calls use Twilio (`server/integrations/twilio.js`).
- **Video:**
  - FFmpeg helpers: `server/video/ffmpeg.js`.
  - Studio exporter with 47 xfade transitions, keyframes and ASS titles: `server/video/studio.js`.
  - **Text animations** (title designer → Entrance/Exit, or Studio → Effects → Text animations for every selected title/caption at once): in = Fade, Pop, Slide up, Drop in, Zoom in, Blur in, Typewriter; out = Fade out, Pop out, Slide down, Zoom out, Blur out, Type away (`text.animIn` / `text.animOut`; `textAnim` in both studio.js files, same numbers; export: `animTags` on the first/last subtitle piece, an exit gets its own last 0.3 s piece, keyframed titles frame by frame). **Typing sound** (`text.typeSound`, default on, `text.typeVol` 0-2; on/off and a Typing volume slider 0-200% in Effect controls for any typewriter title, and in the title designer; each change plays a click at that volume): a key click per letter (softer typing away), synthesized in code: preview with Web Audio as letters appear during playback (`typeClick`, `typedCount`), export as one WAV per title (`typingTrack` + `typeHits`) mixed in at the title's start. Tested: export types the letters, pops out at the end, clicks in the sound. A new title is selected right after it's added. **Motion** presets (Effects → Motion: Slow zoom in / out = scale keys 100→115% over the clip, Punch-in = 118% for 1.5 s at the playhead). **Markers**: M adds/removes one at the playhead (`seq.markers`), ; and ' jump to the previous/next, click one on the ruler to jump, right-click removes; the playhead and clips snap to them.
  - **Effect layers** (like Premiere's adjustment layers; clip `type: 5`, `FX_LAYER` in studio.js): Studio → Effects → New effect layer, or pick any effect with nothing selected; it's placed over the whole edit on the top video track (a new "Effects" track if that's busy) and selected. Its effects apply to everything on the tracks below it while it's on the timeline (trim it to cover part of the edit); titles and captions are never affected. Opacity sets the layer's strength. Preview: `drawFxLayer` copies the canvas drawn so far, runs it through the layer's filters and retro looks, and lays it back at that opacity. Export (`fxLayer` in `server/video/studio.js`): the composite so far is split, the layer's span trimmed, filtered (effect time from the layer's start, like the preview), given alpha and overlaid back. Tested: Noir + VHS layer at 80% over clips on two tracks, untouched before and after.
  - **Preview playback** (`Monitor`): the playback loop owns the time and updates the editor's playhead ~15 times a second (it was every frame, re-drawing the whole editor); small drift between players is evened out with playback speed (±8%) and only jumps over 0.6 s seek; a buffering video holds the clock; a video still seeking keeps the last picture on screen (up to 0.7 s) instead of flashing black, and pausing hands back the exact played time and only seeks if off by more than half a frame. The old code went black after pausing and on every frame step (reproduced, fixed).
  - **Retro looks** (Studio → Effects → Retro, like CapCut's filters): VHS, 90s Camcorder, Y2K Digicam, Super 8, Old Film, Glitch, CRT TV, Noir, Night Vision, 8-bit, each with Intensity % (`mix`, 1-100: the look laid over the untouched picture; export blends a copy with `split` + `blend`, the preview draws the plain picture then the look at that alpha), Strength (`amount`) (and a Date stamp switch on VHS/Camcorder/Digicam: PLAY + date, REC + tape counter + date, orange digicam date, in VT323). Export: plain FFmpeg filter chains in `server/video/retro.js` (rgbashift, noise, drawgrid scanlines, drawbox tracking band/scratch/glitch blocks, curves, colorbalance, drawtext); preview: `renderer/js/lib/retro.js` (CSS filters, an SVG RGB-split filter, grain tile, scanlines, the same bands/stamps) with the same numbers. Stamps and flashes go in the part of the clip that's on screen (`onScreen` in studio.js; a wide video filling a tall frame has its sides off screen). Written from scratch, no third-party code.
  - AI edit operations: `server/video/edit-ops.js`.
  - Preview proxies: `server/video/proxy.js`.
  - Looking through video: `server/video/vision.js` (`look`, `cached`, `summarize`, `shotAt`). FFmpeg finds the shots, grabs a still per shot, measures brightness, sharpness and motion; faces are counted; an AI that can see (`ai.ask({ images })`, Claude/GPT/Grok, or the free `llama3.2-vision` via Ollama) describes each shot (kind, what, on-screen text, quality, usable). Runs in the `analyze` job (setting `video.lookThrough`) and on demand (`video.look`, Production → Footage → Look through / Shots); feeds clip discovery and the speech ads' B-roll and line picking.
- **Copy a video's style** (a small option in Growth → Politics → Package settings; `server/video/reverse.js`, job `video.reverse`, `/api/reverse`, `/api/reverse/upload`): any video link or uploaded file. Measures cuts, pacing, colours and loudness, looks through it shot by shot, and an AI that can see writes a recipe. ## Production health
- **Lag fix** (`server/core/store.js`): the whole database used to be stringified, fsynced and copied to `.bak` every 200 ms while anything changed (constantly during production), inside the process the app talks to. Now: at most every 2 s, written in the background (`flushAsync`, write-then-rename with a generation check so an older background write never overwrites a newer one), `.bak` every 5 min; `flush()` stays synchronous for shutdown and backups. The scheduler prunes hourly (finished jobs older than a week beyond the newest 400; logs of finished jobs older than a day trimmed to 5 lines), and the outreach guide keeps research page text in `<data>/political/<id>/research.json` instead of on `prospect.kit` (the database keeps titles and links).
- Heavy jobs (renders, ads, analysis, local transcription) wait while RAM is low: the reserve is `resources.reserveRamMb` (default 1000) capped at 12% of the PC's RAM. It used to be a fixed 6 GB, which blocked every ordinary PC.
- `server/setup/production.js`: `health()` lists blockers (paused queue, Lite mode, remote-only, FFmpeg/yt-dlp missing, no speech-to-text, no AI, jobs waiting, last failure) with one-click fixes; shown by `renderer/js/modules/health.js` on Production and Politics. `installFfmpeg` (job `setup.ffmpeg`) installs FFmpeg + yt-dlp via winget and restarts stuck imports. `ff.detect()` re-scans for tools.
- Clip cards have an × (on hover) that deletes the clip after a confirmation (`DELETE /api/clips/:id`: cancels its renders, removes its approvals, unpublished post and render files; footage stays).
- Every footage row opens a panel (`/api/assets/:id/detail`) with its steps, jobs, clips, Retry (`/process`), Cancel and Delete (`/cancel`). Failed jobs write `asset.error`.

## Captions and transcripts
- The `transcribe` job installs the free Whisper itself when no speech-to-text exists; the app-only bat also installs it (`Install Extras.bat whisper`). After a transcript arrives, clips in review that were rendered without captions are re-rendered.
- Full transcript per video: Production → footage panel → Transcript (copy, `.txt`, `.srt` via `/api/assets/:id/transcript-file/:fmt`), `POST /api/assets/:id/transcribe`.
- **Caption timing (Nov 4 2026, after Ryan: Nov 3's clips were in sync, the revert's were all off):** word times are whisper.cpp's precise DTW times read from its full JSON (`-ojf`, `wordsFromWhisper`, `timing: 'dtw' | 'rough'`), and the loudness alignment is OFF (`align.alignFile` runs only with `{ force }` or `ai.transcription.align`). Whisper is tried with DTW + full JSON, then steps back one option at a time and remembers only what actually failed (`noDtw` / `noFullJson`). Local transcripts made with the old timing (no `timing`) are transcribed again once (`intelligence.needsRedo`; `handlers.alignedTranscript` shares one run per video across its clips). Per video, footage panel → Transcript → Caption timing: Exact (default) or Shifted to the loudness (`asset.captionTiming: 'aligned'`, `POST /api/assets/:id/caption-timing`, re-renders that video's unpublished clips). `loadTranscript` uses `wordsRaw` (Whisper's own times, kept when alignment runs) unless a video is set to 'aligned'. An automatic per-video sync checker (voice-band loudness, steady-offset search, then phrase alignment) was built and tested on the 12 best shorts with their burned-in captions as ground truth: it rated the right timing best in only 4 of 12 and moved one further off, so it was NOT shipped; loudness can't reliably tell speech under music/noise. The whole music library stays in automatic picks (composed variations and Scary included); music is always its own layer, mixed after the voice cleanup and ducked under the voice (`music.scoreVideo` sidechain).
- Caption look per clip (`clip.style`, over `client.style`; sanitized by `pipeline.sanitizeStyle`): clip panel → "Captions and text" with live preview, apply to this clip or the whole client. Captions and the opening banner (`hook*` keys: `hookFont`, `hookSize`, `hookPosition`, `hookBox`, `hookOutline`, `hookOutlineWidth`, `hookTilt`, `hookAnim`) are styled independently. Tested on a 2-minute sample: 46 s of talk condensed to 8 strong lines ending on "We have to see what is behind that door." **Story text** (from a 6M-view exploring video): no voice; `discover` makes `clipsPerFootage` clips of `kind: 'story'` (`pipeline.createStoryClips`, each with its own `window`, a slice of the video) rendered by `renderBroll` → `brolledit.make({ raw, window, cutBeats: 2.6, texts, strict })`: raw footage (no grade or push-in), straight cuts on the beat, `storyMood` music (default suspense), `storySeconds` (default 45), and text lines (`server/video/story.js`: AI writes 4-10 lines from the shots and transcript, hook ending in "...", escalating, last a question ending "??"; template fallback varies by part; `version.story.lines`) in TikTok's plain text look (TikTok Sans, white, soft blurred shadow, upper third), changing on cuts. **Custom** keeps the client's own settings. **Mix per video** (`client.production.styleMix`, e.g. `{ walkthrough: 2, documentary: 2, story: 2 }`, − / + counters under Video style; overrides the Clips slider): the `discover` job runs one pass per style with that style's own recipe (Documentary: 22-40 s clips, cut-ins on via `clip.visualCuts`, no zoom punch-ins; other styles: the client's lengths and zooms; `discoverClips({ minSec, maxSec })`), excluding what earlier passes took (before, the mix only copied each style's text look, so Documentary clips came out with the client's lengths and zooms and lost their feel); each clip gets `clip.style` = the preset's look, `clip.videoStyle`, and the preset's reframe, set at creation so the render never starts in the wrong look, and makes the story edits alongside (`createStoryClips`). Tested: 2/2/2 on one video made Walkthrough, Documentary, Walkthrough (only 3 talking moments in a 28 s test video) + 2 Story edits, each with its own look. **Titles always fit** (`buildAss`/`fitHook`): a too-wide title is split into 2 balanced lines and, if still too wide, shrunk (`\fs`) to 93% of the width (a third line only if two would drop below 70% of the size); lines you broke yourself are kept. **Titles are short hooks, never quotes** (`intelligence.cleanHook` / `topicHook`): the AI is told to write a 2-5 word title about the subject ("Abandoned factory...", "Why did they leave it all behind..."), not the speaker's words; any title that quotes what is said in the clip (its first 3 words appear in the clip's speech) becomes a topic title from the video's name ("Inside The Town Evacuated From a Sinkhole" → "Town Evacuated..."), and one over 7 words / 40 characters is cut at a natural break (comma, then because/but/when..., then a preposition) with "...". Applied when clips are found and again in `render.short` (so old clips are fixed on re-render; short typed titles are kept). `brolledit.plan` never repeats footage: shots are split into back-to-back pieces, middle pieces first, a second piece of a shot only 1.5 s+ away, and when footage runs out the edit ends early instead of repeating; pieces never reach into the video's last 0.4 s, and after rendering `render` checks the picture's real length (`videoLength`, counted frames): if a shot came out short (the picture ended before the music, so players froze on the last frame, up to ~15 s), the edit is cut where the picture ends with the music faded there, and `make` shortens the grid to match; story edits never use another edit's current shots (`strict`) and widen their window when short.
- **Music in Studio** (`POST /api/clips/:id/studio`): the music a render added is never in the footage, so Studio puts it on its own **Music** audio track (`exporters.musicClips`, from `version.music.cues` which now keep `file`, `offset`, `vol` and `version.music.volume`; older versions find the file in `<data>/music/<mood>/<track>`), movable, trimmable and deletable. ## Crash safety
- Each page is wrapped in `PageGuard` (`renderer/js/main.js`, a Preact error boundary): an error while drawing one page shows "This page hit a problem" on that page (Try again / Go to Messages, which also forgets the remembered Production client) instead of taking down the app. `boot` only shows "Could not reach the Euclidean engine" when `/api/bootstrap` actually fails.

6. **Automation:** paste names or run a scout, and each lead becomes a background job with progress and re-runnable steps, per-hour sending caps, opt-out handling and reply tracking. Show it on a Political screen on the desktop and in the phone app.
