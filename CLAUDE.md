# Euclidean

Desktop app for Euclidean Media (founders Ryan Rosario and Ben Gingold), a short-form video agency for creators and political campaigns. It runs clients, footage, clip production, approvals, messaging, publishing, analytics and AI agents in one place.

## How it's built
- **Electron shell:** `app/main.js` and `app/preload.js`. The engine runs inside it; `ctx` hooks include `pickFolder`, `pickFiles`, `relaunch` and `revealFile`.
- **Node engine:** `server/index.js` and `server/api.js` (a custom Router: `r.get/post/patch/del(path, (req, body, params) => ..., { roles, stream, raw })`).
  - JSON store: `server/core/store.js` (`ctx.store.all/get/find/insert/update/remove`).
  - Audit log: `server/core/audit.js`.
  - Server-sent events: `server/core/events.js`.
- **Jobs:** `server/jobs/queue.js` and `server/jobs/handlers.js`. Every automated step is a `queue.register(name, { title, run(job, h) })` handler; `h.progress(frac, text)` reports progress.
- **AI cost order (default, set once on existing installs via `_costModeV1`):** `ai.local = 'all'` and `ai.bridge.use = 'first'`: the founder's own Grok/Claude/ChatGPT login (rate-limited, only once logged in on the AI apps page), then free Llama, then paid API keys.
- **AI:** `server/integrations/ai.js`. Use `ai.ask({ role, system, prompt, json, search, clientId, jobId })`; it routes by role to Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI with fallback, budgets and usage tracking. `search: true` gives live web search on xAI only.
- **Messaging:** `server/automation/messaging.js`.
  - `createOutbound(...)` puts AI drafts and first contact with prospects into Approvals.
  - `deliver` re-checks opt-outs, quiet hours, the hourly cap and Test mode before sending.
  - In Test mode, mail goes to the sandbox address.
  - Email is SMTP/IMAP (`server/integrations/email.js`); texts and calls use Twilio (`server/integrations/twilio.js`).
- **Video:**
  - FFmpeg helpers: `server/video/ffmpeg.js`.
  - Studio exporter with 47 xfade transitions, keyframes and ASS titles: `server/video/studio.js`.
  - AI edit operations: `server/video/edit-ops.js`.
  - Preview proxies: `server/video/proxy.js`.
  - Looking through video: `server/video/vision.js` (`look`, `cached`, `summarize`, `shotAt`). FFmpeg finds the shots, grabs a still per shot, measures brightness, sharpness and motion; faces are counted; an AI that can see (`ai.ask({ images })`, Claude/GPT/Grok, or the free `llama3.2-vision` via Ollama) describes each shot (kind, what, on-screen text, quality, usable). Runs in the `analyze` job (setting `video.lookThrough`) and on demand (`video.look`, Production → Footage → Look through / Shots); feeds clip discovery and the speech ads' B-roll and line picking.
- **Copy a video's style** (a small option in Growth → Politics → Package settings; `server/video/reverse.js`, job `video.reverse`, `/api/reverse`, `/api/reverse/upload`): any video link or uploaded file. Measures cuts, pacing, colours and loudness, looks through it shot by shot, and an AI that can see writes a recipe. "Use for the political ads" sets `political.styleNotes` (fed to the ad writer) and the pacing (`political.reference`).
- **Your AI apps** (sidebar "AI apps", `/bridge`; search bar "Ask Grok/Claude/ChatGPT", `@grok …`): talk to Grok, Claude and ChatGPT with the founders' own logins, no API keys. `app/bridge.js` (Electron main, passed to the engine as `ctx.bridge`) opens each site in a hidden window with its own saved login (`persist:bridge-<site>`), types the message and reads the reply once it stops changing. Engine side: `server/integrations/bridge.js` (history, one message at a time). Optional `ai.bridge.use` = off | first | fallback routes Euclidean's own AI work through the account as provider `bridge` (max `ai.bridge.perHour`, no images). Personal-account terms limit automated use, so it's off by default.
- **GrokBots by email** (`server/integrations/grokbots.js`, AI apps page → GrokBots): founders add their GrokBots' email addresses (`grokbots.list`); "Email the task" sends it from the outreach mailbox tagged `[JEV-<id>]`; the bot's reply is matched in `messaging.receive` and saved on `botTasks` (not treated as a client message). No API keys.
- **Agent network:** `server/agents/` (registry, adapters, broker, protocol, routes). Peer-to-peer agents; nobody is the central orchestrator; external actions need human approval.
- **Creators analytics:** `server/integrations/creators.js` (yt-dlp, Social Blade-style report).
- **Renderer:** Preact + htm with no build step, in `renderer/js`. Modules are in `renderer/js/modules/*.js` (studio, analytics, growth, agents, settings, production and others).
  - Light theme by default: white base, pastel accents and buttons. Night mode (Settings → Appearance → Night, `html[data-night]`, `renderer/css/night.css`) is a red-on-black cyberpunk HUD: black bezel around a black workspace, hairline red panels with red header bars and folder tabs, Chakra Petch + Share Tech Mono fonts, grayscale thumbnails.
- **Phone app:** `mobile/`, an Expo SDK 57 React Native remote for the engine.
  - Run it with `Start Phone App.bat` (Expo Go).
  - `.github/workflows/ios-app.yml` builds an unsigned `.ipa` for Sideloadly.
- **Launchers:** `Start Euclidean.bat` installs Node, FFmpeg and yt-dlp, runs `npm install`, creates a desktop shortcut and starts the app. `Build Installer.bat` runs `npm run dist`. `.bat` files must keep CRLF line endings.

## Production health
- Heavy jobs (renders, ads, analysis, local transcription) wait while RAM is low: the reserve is `resources.reserveRamMb` (default 1000) capped at 12% of the PC's RAM. It used to be a fixed 6 GB, which blocked every ordinary PC.
- `server/setup/production.js`: `health()` lists blockers (paused queue, Lite mode, remote-only, FFmpeg/yt-dlp missing, no speech-to-text, no AI, jobs waiting, last failure) with one-click fixes; shown by `renderer/js/modules/health.js` on Production and Politics. `installFfmpeg` (job `setup.ffmpeg`) installs FFmpeg + yt-dlp via winget and restarts stuck imports. `ff.detect()` re-scans for tools.
- Clip cards have an × (on hover) that deletes the clip after a confirmation (`DELETE /api/clips/:id`: cancels its renders, removes its approvals, unpublished post and render files; footage stays).
- Every footage row opens a panel (`/api/assets/:id/detail`) with its steps, jobs, clips, Retry (`/process`), Cancel and Delete (`/cancel`). Failed jobs write `asset.error`.

## Captions and transcripts
- The `transcribe` job installs the free Whisper itself when no speech-to-text exists; the app-only bat also installs it (`Install Extras.bat whisper`). After a transcript arrives, clips in review that were rendered without captions are re-rendered.
- Full transcript per video: Production → footage panel → Transcript (copy, `.txt`, `.srt` via `/api/assets/:id/transcript-file/:fmt`), `POST /api/assets/:id/transcribe`.
- Caption look per clip (`clip.style`, over `client.style`; sanitized by `pipeline.sanitizeStyle`): clip panel → "Captions and text" with live preview, apply to this clip or the whole client. Captions and the opening banner (`hook*` keys: `hookFont`, `hookSize`, `hookPosition`, `hookBox`, `hookOutline`, `hookOutlineWidth`, `hookTilt`, `hookAnim`) are styled independently. Quick looks (TikTok, TikTok plain, Instagram, Instagram plain, Cartoon, Comic, Bubbly, Big & loud, Clean) come in two rows, Caption looks and Title looks, each applying only its half. Captions and the title are fully separate: a title still on "same as captions" keeps its font/size when the captions change (frozen in `CaptionStyle.setS`), and it has its own shadow (`hookShadow`). In Studio, captions carry `isCaption` (set by `toVelo` and Add captions; older projects are marked from the Captions track by `markCaptions`), so titles you add yourself are never touched by caption controls. TikTok looks use TikTok Sans (OFL, bundled as fixed-weight instances "TikTok Sans" 600 and "TikTok Sans Bold" 800); Instagram looks use Inter (Instagram Sans isn't free to use; Inter is the closest free match). Title boxes can be rounded (`hookRadius`): drawn as shapes sized from the font's own measurements (`fontnames.measure`, `captions.roundRect`), in renders, Studio preview and Studio export. Titles are fully on screen from the first frame (no fade-in; "Bounce in" is a pulse from full size), since that frame is the cover on TikTok and Reels. About 60 bundled fonts (OFL/Apache, licences in `server/video/fonts/licenses`) grouped in `renderer/js/lib/fonts.js` (Bold YouTube, Horror, Handwritten, Retro/gaming, Classic, System); fonts without a real bold are drawn at their own weight (`captions.HEAVY`). Caption rotation (`rotate`) and banner rotation (`hookTilt`), sizes 30-220. The banner text is the clip's `hook` (multi-line), edited in the panel. "Edit caption words" (`GET/PUT /api/clips/:id/lines`) rewrites the footage transcript for that range, keeping timing.
- **Clip engine** (`server/video/montage.js`): finds narrator voiceover from the audio (quiet gaps between words, or speech over shots with nobody on screen; level map cached as `<file>.levels.json`), drone/aerial shots (vision kind `aerial`) and strong shots, then scores sentence-aligned windows (voiceover, drone, visuals, cuts, peaks, hook line; filler, outros, dark/unusable shots and dead air count against). Story cuts (off by default: `production.storyCuts` / `client.production.storyCuts`; they made edits choppy and drifted captions when pieces overlapped, now merged in `renderShort`): windows up to ~2.2x the max length are condensed to whole sentences (`montage.condense`: keeps the opening hook and the ending, drops filler/outros and the weakest lines), stored as `clip.keep` and rendered as those parts only (`plan.keep`); ending on a reveal (`END_HOOK`) scores higher. Reaction bursts (`montage.reactionBursts`: moments 3+ robust deviations louder than the ~20 s around them, from the level map) add a small bonus. Ideas (not code) from VideoHighlighter, which is AGPL-3.0: never copy its files in. Clips per video: `client.production.clipsPerFootage` (slider by the upload box). `discoverClips` sends the top candidates to the AI to choose and title (method `ai`), else takes the best scores (method `scored`). Renders lay in **visual cuts** (`planCovers` + `assemble`): weak or static stretches get 2-3 s of the best shots from elsewhere (taken from the middle of the shot) while the voice keeps going; every cut-away in a clip is a different shot (never the same shot, its neighbour within 3 s, or one described the same way; cut-aways stop when good shots run out). **Variety across clips** (`elsewhere` in `planCovers`/`coverBlack`, from `handlers.coversElsewhere`): shots the video's other clips already use (their `coverSrcs`, saved when planned and again with each version, or `version.covers`, plus B-roll edit segs) go to the back of the line: unused shots first (best first), then the least-used; a reused long shot gives the stretch farthest from what was used. Before, every clip of a video picked the same top drone shot. **B-roll dealt up front** (`handlers.brollPile`, `mine` in `planCovers`): the video's good cut-away shots are dealt to its clips in turn (never two from the same scene to one clip), each clip uses its own pile first, so clips get different drone/B-roll whatever order they render in (tested: 6 clips rendered in reverse with no shared info, zero shared footage). `coverBlack` keeps 3 s away from shots the clip already uses. Captions stay on the audio through cut-aways and jump cuts (tested within one frame); true black fades (`blackIntervals`, strict thresholds, at most 2 per clip) are covered (`clip.visualCuts`, `client.production.visualCuts`). **Black check before rendering** (`montage.blackInRange` → `dips`: decodes only the clip's own seconds at 10 fps, brightness per frame; a stretch counts only if it reaches near-black (YAVG < 24; black is ~16, dark rooms and dark reveal shots stay above ~40, so they're kept), then it's widened over the whole fade down and back up (neighbours under 0.9x the brightness around them), ≤ 6 s; tested: a fade 4.0-7.5 s found exactly, zero hits on both reference videos): black stretches inside the clip are covered with `coverBlack` before the first render, so clips render once. **Black-frame guard** (safety net after rendering, in `render.short`): the finished clip is scanned for near-black stretches (`blackIntervals(..., { loose: true, cache: false })`: pix_th 0.10, pic_th 0.90, ≥0.25 s); the finished clip is checked with the same `blackInRange`; each is mapped back to source time through the jump cuts (`outputToSource`) and covered at any length with good picture from elsewhere (`coverBlack`: vision shots, else scene-cut pieces; never dark or reused; each candidate is checked on the spot with `darkAt` instead of scanning the whole source video, which was slow on long footage), then the clip is re-assembled and re-rendered (up to 2 passes). Works without Look through too.
- **Ad reads are never clipped** (`server/video/ads.js`, `adRanges(words)`, in the `discover` job): sentences with sponsor/website/merch/code/".com"/link-in-description/like-and-subscribe cues (plus a plain line between two ad lines, or "go check it out" next to one) are merged into ranges, saved on `asset.ads`, added to `exclude`, and any pick overlapping an ad by over 1 s is dropped (Stringer plugs his website in every intro).
- **Speed**: libx264 uses `production.x264Preset` (default veryfast, CRF one lower) instead of medium (about 30% faster renders in tests); `analysis.analyze` reads scene cuts and loudness side by side; `vision.look` reuses the analyze step's scene cuts (`cuts`, no second full read of the video) and grabs stills/measurements four at a time (look-through 23.7 s → 13.2 s on an 87 s video).
- **Sound per clip** (clip panel → "Sound and music"): voice volume (`clip.voiceVol` 0-2), music volume (`clip.musicVol`), mood chips (`clip.music`), or a specific track from the catalog (`clip.musicTrack` = `own|lib|gen/<mood>/<file>`; `/api/music/catalog`, `/api/music/play?id=`, job `music.fill` via `/api/music/fill`). Music cues come from `server/video/score.js`; mixing and ducking in `music.scoreVideo`.
- **Transcript cleanup** (`server/video/transcript-fix.js`, applied on load and saved back): first/last words of a phrase that soaked up a pause are cut back to 2x the typical word length; Whisper repetition loops collapse. Transcribe adds the file's audio start offset (`probe().audioOffset`) and runs whisper.cpp with `--dtw` when supported. Pause cuts only happen where the audio is actually quiet (`keptSegments(..., silences)`). Jump cuts use `settb` + `setpts=N` so the final fps step never repeats frames.
- **Caption timing** (`captions.timedChunks`, used by renders, `.srt`, Studio Add captions and `toVelo`): lines never overlap (Whisper word times overlap a little and lines were held at least 0.25 s, so with 2 words per line two lines stacked and jumped); each word ends when the next starts, each line ends when the next begins, and pauses under 0.3 s keep the line up. Cutting a caption in Studio (`splitWords` in `splitAt`) gives each half only its own words, timed from its own start.
- **Highlight** (`style.highlightMode`): `word` (spoken word), `keywords` (chosen words in `clip.keywords`, or auto-picked by `captions.autoKeywords`: numbers, names, strong words) or `none`; keyword picker in the captions panel. Renders and Studio exports use `<data>/fonts.conf` (fontconfig) so FFmpeg finds the bundled fonts on Windows. Bundled fonts are written into subtitles by their own full name (`server/video/fontnames.js` reads it from the file, e.g. "Montserrat ExtraBold"), never the bare family, because a same-named font installed in Windows (Adobe installs Montserrat) otherwise wins and exports come out thinner than the preview. **Size matching** (`fontnames.emFit`): the browser's font size is the em, but libass sizes text by the font's Windows ascent+descent (1.3-1.8x the em), so the same number drew much smaller in exports. Studio export multiplies by `k` and shifts the baseline by `dy` to match the canvas, puts each line in its own event 1.15x the size apart (like the preview) rotated around the title centre (`\org`), and draws boxes as shapes with the preview's geometry (keyframed boxed titles: all boxes first, then text). `toVelo` converts clip sizes to Studio (em) sizes, and the clip panel preview divides by `k` (`/api/fonts/scale`), so both show the rendered size. Tested: preview and export bounding boxes within ~4 px for Montserrat, Anton, TikTok Sans, Inter.
- **Studio captions:** Studio projects made from a clip carry each caption's word timings (`text.words`) and highlight (`text.hlMode` word|keywords, `text.hlColor`, `text.keywords`); the captions bar above the timeline has Highlight (Off / Spoken word / Keywords, colour, "Pick keywords for me" via `POST /api/captions/keywords`, click words to toggle). Preview (`drawText`) and export (`server/video/studio.js`, one subtitle event per spoken word) draw it the same way. Zoom: pinch or Ctrl+wheel on the timeline (around the mouse; plain wheel scrolls it) and on the preview (up to 800%, drag the grey area or middle button to pan, double-click empty space or Fit to reset); touch screens pinch too. Double-click a title on the preview to edit it. Clicking a caption selects just that one (Shift/Ctrl adds; "Select all captions" picks them all); captions slide along their own track without pushing others to another layer; dragging one of several selected titles/captions on the preview moves or resizes them all by the same amount. Renaming: the clip name field at the top of Effect controls, F2, or right-click → Rename (`clip.renamed`; titles and captions then show the name instead of their text; the opening title keeps `isHook`). Click the project name in the header (or right-click a project in the list) to rename it; `PUT /api/projects/:id` with only a name keeps the contents. Premiere keys: Ctrl+B (or Shift+S) cuts every track at the playhead, Ctrl+A selects everything, drag on empty timeline space box-selects, Q / W delete everything before / after the playhead on every track (Q moves the rest to 0:00), Alt/Shift+Delete ripple deletes (a selected block with nothing else in its time span closes that span on all tracks at once, so captions stay in sync), and deleting the start of the edit moves everything up to 0:00. Timeline drags end when the mouse moves with no button held (a fast click used to leave a drag stuck and drop the clip on another track). **Add captions** (captions bar, always shown): `POST /api/studio/captions` makes caption pieces with word timings from the transcript of each clip on the audio tracks (or video clips); videos with no transcript are transcribed first (`POST /api/studio/transcribe`: the footage's own `transcribe` job, or job `studio.transcribe` for files not in Production, kept in `<data>/studio/transcripts`), and the bar waits for them. **Captions look** and **Title look** apply any clip-panel quick look (`LOOKS`, exported from `clip.js`) to the captions or to the Hook title separately. With several titles/captions selected, Effect controls shows `MultiTextControls` (font, size, outline, shadow, colours, box, rounded corners, capitals, TikTok/Instagram looks) and "Style all N" opens the title designer, whose save copies the look (`STYLE_KEYS`, not the words) to all of them. Title designer presets include TikTok, TikTok plain and Instagram, with a Rounded corners slider; font pickers list every bundled font (`fontOptions`). **YouTube link** (header button, or Ctrl+V a YouTube link anywhere in Studio outside a text box): `POST /api/studio/link` downloads the audio with yt-dlp, converts it to MP3 in `<data>/studio/links` (named after the video title; the same link is downloaded once, `<sha1>.json` remembers it), and `addLink` imports it and places it at the playhead, full length, 100% volume, on the first audio track free there (else a new one). **Music** (header button, `MusicLibrary`): moods from `/api/music/catalog`, Listen plays `/api/music/play`, Add resolves the file (`POST /api/music/file`), imports it and places it at the playhead on a Music audio track at 25% volume, trimmed to the edit, with fades.
- **B-roll edit** (off by default; `client.production.brollEdit`, `brollMood` default darkbeat, `brollSeconds` default 20; toggle by the upload box): after Make clips, each video also gets a clip with `kind: 'broll'` (`pipeline.createBrollClip`; or on demand from the footage panel, `POST /api/assets/:id/broll`). `render.short` hands it to `renderBroll` → `server/video/brolledit.js`: the best shots (vision shots by `shotScore` + drone bonus, else scene-cut pieces; never the same moment or description twice, no black) in story order, each from the middle of its shot with a slow push-in, moody grade, 0.4 s crossfades, original audio dropped, mood music (`music.pick` or the clip's chosen track) with fades and loudness. **Beat sync** (`server/video/beats.js`: tempo by autocorrelation of bass+full-band onsets, beat phase, cached `<track>.beats.json`; `cutGrid` cuts every 1/2/4/8 beats near 2 s with a double cut every 4th shot): `brolledit.make` cuts on the beats, mostly hard cuts with every third a flash/zoom/slide/blur (`beatTransitions`, centred on the beat; `durations` sizes the shots), alternating push-in/pull-out; without a beat it falls back to 2.4 s crossfades. Music mood **Dark beat** (`darkbeat`: Openverse searches dark ambient beat / horror trap / dark phonk; the generated version is a drone with kick and snare at 92 BPM). Shots used by the footage's earlier B-roll edits are avoided when possible (`version.broll.segs`). **One per day** (`production.brollDaily`, scheduler `dailyBroll`: the footage with the fewest B-roll edits) and **Make a B-roll edit now** (`POST /api/clients/:id/broll`) by the upload box. B-roll edits go to review like any clip.
- **Video styles per client** (Production upload box → Video style; `server/video/styles.js`, `GET /api/video-styles`, `POST /api/clients/:id/video-style`, `client.production.videoStyle`): picking one copies its caption look and production settings onto that client only. **Documentary** (from the best-performing Stringer short): Montserrat ExtraBold captions 2 words per line, sentence case, white with key words yellow #F8D800 (`highlightMode: keywords`), size 150 (matched to the reference within a pixel of width), all-caps title Montserrat 215 with a black outline and shadow at the top for 6 s (long one-line titles wrap into two balanced lines, `buildAss`), face-following full-screen crop. **Story text** (from a 6M-view exploring video): no voice; `discover` makes `clipsPerFootage` clips of `kind: 'story'` (`pipeline.createStoryClips`, each with its own `window`, a slice of the video) rendered by `renderBroll` → `brolledit.make({ raw, window, cutBeats: 2.6, texts, strict })`: raw footage (no grade or push-in), straight cuts on the beat, `storyMood` music (default suspense), `storySeconds` (default 45), and text lines (`server/video/story.js`: AI writes 4-10 lines from the shots and transcript, hook ending in "...", escalating, last a question ending "??"; template fallback varies by part; `version.story.lines`) in TikTok's plain text look (TikTok Sans, white, soft blurred shadow, upper third), changing on cuts. **Custom** keeps the client's own settings. `brolledit.plan` never repeats footage: shots are split into back-to-back pieces, middle pieces first, a second piece of a shot only 1.5 s+ away, and when footage runs out the edit ends early instead of repeating; story edits never use another edit's current shots (`strict`) and widen their window when short.
- **Both sizes** (`clip.wide`; the **Dual video** switch next to Upload sets `client.production.wide`, and switching it also updates that client's clips still waiting to render): the render also makes a full-frame 16:9 version (`renderShort({ wide: true })`: 1920×1080, fit, same cuts, captions scaled 0.7, same music), saved as `version.wide` and served by `/api/clips/:id/wide`. The clip player (`ClipViews`) switches Vertical / Full frame / Both (Both opens a large side-by-side view); without one it offers "Also make full-frame (16:9)".
- Clip length per client (`client.production.minClipSec/maxClipSec`): sliders by the upload box; used by `discoverClips`.
- **Make clips / AI tips** (upload box): new footage imports, transcribes and analyses, then waits (`asset.status = 'waiting'`, `handlers.discoverOrWait`) until **Make clips** is pressed (the pressed-down button above Upload, or on the footage row), so clip length, count and tips can still change; `client.production.waitForStart` (default on; autopilot clients never wait). `/api/assets/:id/process` sets `asset.started` (pressed while the video is still importing/transcribing/analysing, it only marks it started and clips are made the moment it's ready). The upload-box button shows as soon as footage is uploaded ("N videos, getting ready"). **Tips for the AI** (`client.production.aiTips`) go into the clip-picking prompt and, without an AI, nudge the scores (`tipNudge`: tip words raise, words after no/skip/avoid lower); an empty box picks the normal way.

## Crash safety
- Each page is wrapped in `PageGuard` (`renderer/js/main.js`, a Preact error boundary): an error while drawing one page shows "This page hit a problem" on that page (Try again / Go to Messages, which also forgets the remembered Production client) instead of taking down the app. `boot` only shows "Could not reach the Euclidean engine" when `/api/bootstrap` actually fails.
- Production ignores a remembered client (`eu.prodClient` in localStorage) that no longer exists; the Video style switch read `client.name` of a deleted/archived client and crashed the whole app at start.

## Clients and Finance editing
- Clients: New client; remove from the list (trash on each row) or the client page (Remove): **Archive** (`DELETE /api/clients/:id?mode=archive`, status `archived`, everything kept) or **Delete for good** (`?mode=delete`: removes the client and its assets, clips, posts, approvals, contacts and portal comments from the store; files stay on disk). Status picker on the client page (active, lead, paused, churned, archived).
- Finance: almost everything is clickable. KPI cards and the Revenue / AI rows take a typed number for the month (`settings.financeOverrides[month].revenue|aiCost`; empty = calculated again; `finance.summary` returns `overrides`, `autoRevenue`, `autoAiCost`); Other expenses adds a cost; tax/retained/payout rows and founder rows open the split; client names open the client, retainers/costs/hours are chips; invoices open `InvoiceModal` (client, number, month, amount, status incl. Cancelled, due date, notes, delete; `POST/PATCH/DELETE /api/invoices`); renewals edit the client's renewal date; logged hours are editable (`PATCH /api/timelogs/:id`); expenses can be removed from their edit box.

## Run and test
- `npm start` runs the Electron app; `npm run engine` runs the engine only (`--data <dir> --port <n>`). The login link with its token is printed in the log.
- To test the UI, drive the engine's web UI with Playwright (Chromium is at `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium` in cloud sessions).
- Check rendered video by grabbing frames with FFmpeg.
- Don't run `npm run selftest` unless asked; the user rejected it as too long.

## Working rules (from Ryan)
- Write for beginners: plain-English errors, no terminal, config or JSON for the user, setup through buttons.
- Commit and push finished work to the working branch. Don't open pull requests unless asked.
- Never send texts or emails, post, or change CRM records or accounts without human approval. The one exception is outreach autopilot, which a founder switches on per lane: drafts that pass every check in `server/outreach/autopilot.js` may send automatically. Contrast/attack ads always need a person. Keep each client's data inside its workspace.

## Outreach offer and tone
- Pitch: two free tailored example videos (one vertical, one horizontal) that they can use free of charge whether or not they talk to us, and the ask "Could you give us 20 minutes?"
- Emails are personal and specific, have one clear ask, make no invented claims, and include an opt-out line. Ryan's email templates are the house style.
- Video style: bubbly, poppy motion graphics drawn entirely in code, tailored to each target.

## Political package system (base built; see status below)
Build it into the existing prospects/Growth module (prospect `type: 'political'` already exists, with `prospect.research`, `prospect.audit` and `/api/prospects/:id/outreach`).

1. **Scout:** find new leads from a plain-English query using OpenStates, FEC, Congress.gov, Wikidata, news and xAI search. Skip duplicates.
2. **Research brief:** every claim has a cited source. Cover office, district, committees, votes and bills, fundraising, recent news, social presence, and a public campaign or press contact.
   - Public, professional information only; no home addresses or family details.
3. **Email:** tailored from Ryan's templates, with the free-videos and 20-minute ask. It goes to the campaign or press contact (not official .gov addresses) and waits in Approvals. Batch approve is allowed; nothing sends automatically.
4. **Package:**
   - Videos: about 40 seconds by default (adjustable to 4 minutes), 1080×1920 and 1920×1080. Scenes are laid out in HTML/CSS, screenshotted by headless Edge/Chromium (or an offscreen Electron window), then animated and joined with FFmpeg (zoom, slide-ins, xfade). Optional narrator voice through OpenAI text-to-speech.
   - A one-page PDF sheet showing both videos, the offer and a booking link.
   - Only real public photos and quotes; never fake the politician's face or voice. Add a small "Concept by Euclidean Media" tag.
5. **Calls:**
   - A call list with one-click Twilio dialing and a tailored script, made by a person.
   - An optional voice note in your own (cloned) voice attached to the email.
   - Cloned-voice AI only for inbound callbacks.
   - **No automated robocalls:** under the TCPA they need prior consent, and the FCC counts AI voices as artificial.
6. **Automation:** paste names or run a scout, and each lead becomes a background job with progress and re-runnable steps, per-hour sending caps, opt-out handling and reply tracking. Show it on a Political screen on the desktop and in the phone app.

API keys to connect: Anthropic (required), xAI (lead search), OpenAI (narrator), api.data.gov (Congress.gov and FEC), OpenStates, Twilio and ElevenLabs (calls/voice). Use a separate outreach email domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC records.

  - Factual scenes show their sources on screen.
  - Contrast ads are off by default (`political.contrastAds`). They need a researched opponent and cited facts, and are never auto-sent.
- **Music** (`server/video/music.js`, `political.musicOn`): one track per package, matched to mood (positive, contrast, speech): the founders' folder first, else a free CC0/CC BY library downloaded once from Openverse (credit added to the end card), else a bed generated with FFmpeg.

- **Free scouting** (`server/political/freescout.js`): Ballotpedia election pages and Wikidata office-holders, no keys or AI; runs before the AI search in `political.scout`.
- **Speech ads** (`political.videoStyle = 'speech'`, `server/political/footage.js`, package step `footage`):
  - Finds public videos of the candidate with no login: links pasted on the lead (`prospect.footageLinks`), public Facebook videos/reels found through web search and their sites, their YouTube channel and YouTube search. Downloads the first 10 minutes of up to `political.footageClips` with yt-dlp.
  - Transcribes (OpenAI Whisper or local whisper.cpp), then AI picks whole sentences the candidate says (never cut inside a sentence or splice words), with short issue titles and a tagline.
  - Each line: face-following crop, B-roll (quiet parts of other videos, background and candidate photos) laid over part of it while their voice continues, word captions, issue title pill, name lower third, footage credit. Campaign-graphics opening title and vote card, music ducked under the voice.
  - Falls back to the graphic ads when fewer than two usable lines are found. Clips not posted by the candidate are marked `own: false` and the email then calls the ads concepts.

  - Daily scout rotates through saved searches.
  - Auto-send has a daily cap with a warm-up ramp.
  - It only auto-sends when the email is confirmed on the person's own pages or comes from an imported list, it isn't a .gov or state address, it isn't on the exclusions list, they haven't opted out or already replied, a mailing address is set (CAN-SPAM), and, for politicians, the package is finished and has no contrast ad.
